Mastering AWS EC2 (Part 2)

Cost Optimization & Purchasing Models

Sushil Yadav 5 min read · Published on AWS Learning Journey

A Hands-On Guide to Spot Instances, Savings Plans, Reserved Instances, Dedicated Hosts, and Capacity Reservations

In Part 1 of this series, we explored the core operational mechanics of AWS EC2: allocating Elastic IPs for persistent public networking, vertically scaling instance sizes on demand, and provisioning, formatting, and mounting Amazon EBS block storage volumes.

While On-Demand pricing offers complete operational flexibility with zero long-term commitment, running 24/7 workloads exclusively on On-Demand instances can lead to unnecessary cloud spend. To align infrastructure costs with workload requirements, AWS provides five distinct compute purchasing models.

1. Spot Instances: Deep Discounts for Fault-Tolerant Compute

Spot Instances let you tap into spare, unused EC2 compute capacity across AWS data centers at steep discounts — up to 90% off standard On-Demand pricing.

How Spot Works Under the Hood

AWS monetizes idle server capacity by offering it as Spot capacity at market-driven rates that adjust gradually based on long-term supply and demand. Because this is surplus compute, AWS reserves the right to reclaim instances whenever On-Demand workloads require that physical capacity.

Spot Instances are ideal for stateless, non-critical, or batch-oriented workloads, including:

  • Containerized batch processing jobs
  • Big data ETL pipelines
  • Background image and video rendering tasks
  • Transient testing and CI/CD development environments
⚠️ Critical Production Rule: Never use Spot Instances for 24/7 mission-critical production workloads. Because AWS can reclaim Spot capacity with only a 2-minute notice (delivered via Amazon EventBridge and EC2 instance metadata) when regional demand spikes, relying on Spot for primary production databases or stateful web apps introduces unacceptable risk.

Configuring Spot Requests

When creating a Spot Request in the AWS Management Console, you define your target operational boundaries:

  • Target Instance Count: Define minimum and maximum bounds (e.g., 1 to 4 instances).
  • Hardware Bounds: Specify acceptable RAM ranges (e.g., 8 GB to 32 GB) and vCPU counts, allowing AWS to fulfill the request using any matching available spare instance type.

2. Savings Plans: Flexible Hourly Spend Commitments

Savings Plans offer discounted compute rates in exchange for committing to a consistent amount of hourly spend (measured in $/hour) for a 1-year or 3-year term.

Savings Plan Categories

  1. Compute Savings Plans: Offer the highest flexibility, automatically applying discounts of up to 66% across any EC2 instance family, size, operating system, region, or compute type (including AWS Fargate and AWS Lambda).
  2. EC2 Instance Savings Plans: Offer higher discount rates (up to 72%) for specific instance families within a selected AWS region (e.g., m5 instances in us-east-1), regardless of OS or instance size.
  3. SageMaker AI Savings Plans: Provide savings up to 64% off On-Demand rates. These plans automatically apply to your SageMaker AI instance usage regardless of instance family, size, and component.

3. Reserved Instances (RIs): Term-Based Capacity & Billing Guarantees

Reserved Instances (RIs) provide significant billing discounts (up to 75% compared to On-Demand) when committing to a 1-year (12-month) or 3-year (36-month) term.

Regional vs. Zonal Scope

  • Regional RIs: When purchased at the Regional level, the reservation applies billing discounts across all Availability Zones in that Region, but does not reserve physical capacity.
  • Zonal RIs: When purchased for a specific Availability Zone, a zonal Reserved Instance reserves physical capacity in that exact Availability Zone.

Offering Classes

  • Standard RIs: Deliver the highest discount rates for steady-state workloads where instance family, OS, and region remain fixed throughout the term.
  • Convertible RIs: Offer slightly lower discounts but allow modifying instance attributes (such as changing instance families from m5 to c5, changing OS, or adjusting tenancy) during the term as workload requirements evolve.

Payment Structures

  • All Upfront: Pay for the full term commitment upfront for the maximum possible discount.
  • Partial Upfront: Pay a portion of the cost upfront, with the remainder billed as discounted monthly recurring charges.
  • No Upfront: Zero initial payment, with the discounted rate billed on an hourly basis throughout the term.

4. Dedicated Hosts: Hardware Compliance & License Optimization

Dedicated Hosts are physical, single-tenant servers fully dedicated to your organization’s exclusive use.

Software Licensing Compliance

Many enterprise software products — such as Oracle Database, Microsoft SQL Server, or Windows Server enterprise editions — require licensing tied to specific physical sockets or CPU cores. Dedicated Hosts give you visibility into the underlying physical socket and core layout, enabling Bring Your Own License (BYOL) compliance strategies.

Financial Benefits

By leveraging existing eligible core- and socket-based software licenses under a BYOL model, organizations avoid paying recurring license premiums bundled into standard On-Demand instance rates.

5. Capacity Reservations: AZ-Specific Capacity Assurances

Capacity Reservations guarantee EC2 compute capacity in a specific Availability Zone (AZ) for present or future dates, independent of financial discount commitments.

Key Characteristics

  1. Guaranteed Capacity: Ensures capacity is physically reserved for your AWS account in your target AZ (e.g., us-east-1a), eliminating the risk of InsufficientInstanceCapacity errors during critical deployments.
  2. Flexible Commitment Terms: AWS provides two start modes:
    • Immediate: Requires zero term commitment. You can provision or cancel the reservation at any time, and billing stops immediately upon cancellation (billed per-second, 60-second minimum).
    • At a Future Date: Allows securing capacity 5 to 120 days ahead of planned events. In exchange, AWS requires a minimum commitment duration: typically 14 days for most general instance types.
  3. Combining with Discounts: Capacity Reservations can be paired with Regional Reserved Instances or Savings Plans to receive financial billing discounts on top of the physical capacity guarantee.

Wrapping Up

Optimizing EC2 compute costs requires strategically layering these purchasing models across your architecture rather than relying on a single option:

  • Leverage Spot Instances for elastic, fault-tolerant background tasks.
  • Anchor steady-state production workloads with Savings Plans or Reserved Instances.
  • Reserve Dedicated Hosts for socket-bound BYOL enterprise licensing.
  • Deploy Capacity Reservations whenever non-negotiable AZ capacity guarantees are essential.
